# Nguyen's Story

My name is Nguyen. I am 35 years old and I come from Hung Yen, a peaceful province in northern Vietnam. I work as a software developer. Every day, I write code, solve problems, and build digital tools that help people.

When I was young, I didn’t know much about computers. But I was curious. I liked to take things apart and understand how they worked. One day, I saw someone build a website. It looked like magic. I wanted to learn how to do that too.

So I started studying programming. At first, it was hard. I didn’t understand many words in English. But I didn’t give up. I watched tutorials, read articles, and practiced every day. Slowly, I got better.

Now, I work with people from different countries. We speak English to share ideas and build software together. I still make mistakes, but I learn from them. I believe that every mistake is a step forward.

I want to improve my English more. That’s why I read stories, listen to podcasts, and talk with friends online. I know that learning a language takes time, but I enjoy the journey.
